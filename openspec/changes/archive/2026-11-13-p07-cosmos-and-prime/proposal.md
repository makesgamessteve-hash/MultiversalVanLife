# Proposal

Phase 7 of 24 of the procedural multiverse roadmap (see `docs/ROADMAP.md`). Depends on: p06.

## Why

The top of the hierarchy decides the rules everything below follows: each universe's physics and metaphysics, and the layout of galaxies, clusters and star systems. Earth must be kept out of generation as the fixed Prime universe.

## What Changes

- Multiverse and universes: law sets (magic source, magic and psionic strength, faster-than-light mode, real or silent gods), age, cosmic eras skeleton, a galaxy density field and notable galaxies.
- Galaxies (morphology, age and heavy-element fields, clusters), clusters (stars, lanes) and star systems (stars, planets, moons, belts).
- The fixed Prime universe `u0` with the Milky Way, the Sun, its planets, the Moon and Earth.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `prime-universe`: The fixed Prime universe that is never generated.

### Modified Capabilities
- `multiverse-generation`: Adds universe metaphysics and cosmic structure.

## Impact

- `gen/levels/multiverse.lua`, `universe.lua`, `galaxy.lua`, `cluster.lua`, `system.lua`, `gen/prime.lua`; plausibility checks and bible sections; game tests over 200 seeds.
