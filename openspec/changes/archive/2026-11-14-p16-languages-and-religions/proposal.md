# Proposal

Phase 16 of 24 of the procedural multiverse roadmap (see `docs/ROADMAP.md`). Depends on: p15.

## Why

Names that mean something and beliefs that grow from what peoples see and live through are the heart of a holistic world. Languages and religions also feed traditions, history and dialogue in later phases.

## What Changes

- Languages per culture: anatomy-filtered sound inventories, syllable structures, a lexicon with meanings and names composed from meanings; daughter languages through sound changes.
- Religions from the sky, the land, biology, magic and history, with deities or principles, doctrines, clergy and sacred sites; real deities in belief universes.
- Generated names replace the provisional syllable names of phase 9 for new places.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `culture-generation`: Languages, language families and religions (traditions, society, technology and architecture follow in phases 17–18).
