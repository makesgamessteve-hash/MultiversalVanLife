# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The garden
The conservatory's door SHALL open onto an endless garden with its own sky: meadows, woods, ponds, orchards, flower
beds, hedges and paths, streamed in chunks around the player; the back of the house SHALL stand in the garden with
the door back inside.

#### Scenario: Through the garden door
- **WHEN** the player walks through the conservatory's door
- **THEN** they stand in the garden under its sky, and the door behind them shows the conservatory

#### Scenario: A long walk
- **WHEN** the player walks two kilometres into the garden
- **THEN** the garden keeps coming and the way back leads to the house
