# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Places in signals, map and codex
Signals SHALL name their place and surroundings from the plan; the van SHALL have a map of the planet and explored regions; the codex SHALL have entries for regions, settlements, roads, rivers and named features.

#### Scenario: A call from a town
- **WHEN** a signal comes from a town
- **THEN** Juniper names the town and where it lies, and the map shows it
