# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Road hops
The player SHALL be able to choose a place on the road network (from a signpost, the map or the console) and the van SHALL drive there along the roads at speed.

#### Scenario: To the next town
- **WHEN** the player picks the next town on a signpost
- **THEN** the van drives there along the road and arrives in the town
