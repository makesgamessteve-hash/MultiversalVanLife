# Proposal

Phase 17 of 24 of the procedural multiverse roadmap (see `docs/ROADMAP.md`). Depends on: p16.

## Why

Cultures come alive through their calendars, festivals, rites and values, and through what they can build. These must follow from their planet, their biology, their religion and later their history.

## What Changes

- Calendars from day, year and moons; holidays from astronomy and commemorations; rites of passage, funerals, food customs and taboos tied to real local conditions and species.
- Values and social structure from species instincts, environment and history.
- A technology graph unlocked by resources, domestication, trade, stability and magic, with magic and psionic traditions.
