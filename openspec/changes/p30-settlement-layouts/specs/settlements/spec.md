# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Layouts by culture
Settlements SHALL be laid out by their people's culture and site, with streets, a centre and lots facing the streets, filled with the buildings of their size, trade and condition.

#### Scenario: Two peoples
- **WHEN** villages of a hierarchical and of a traditional people are generated
- **THEN** the first has a planned grid around a central hall, the second organic lanes

#### Scenario: A river town
- **WHEN** a town lies on a river
- **THEN** it has a bridge or ford, a water mill and docks where its technology allows
