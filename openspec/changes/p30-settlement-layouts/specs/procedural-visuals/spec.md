# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Building kinds
The building generator SHALL provide row houses, shops, workshops, inns, granaries, barns, stables, mills, schools, halls, palaces, towers, walls, gates, bridges, docks, warehouses, wells and fountains in each culture's style.

#### Scenario: A market town
- **WHEN** a market town is generated
- **THEN** its centre has shops, an inn and stalls around a square
