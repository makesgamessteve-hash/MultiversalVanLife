# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: People going about their day
Settlements SHALL show people in numbers and activities that follow their population, the time of day, the weather and holidays, walking routines between homes, work, markets and temples.

#### Scenario: Day and night
- **WHEN** the player visits a village at midday and at midnight
- **THEN** the square is busy at midday and nearly empty at midnight, with a few guards and lamplighters

#### Scenario: Talking to anyone
- **WHEN** the player talks to a person in a crowd
- **THEN** they answer in their culture's way and are remembered as a character
