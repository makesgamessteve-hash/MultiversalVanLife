# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Things of daily life
Settlements SHALL be furnished with street furniture, market goods, carts, everyday objects, graves, shrines and banners chosen from their people's technology, customs and faith, and decorated on their holidays.

#### Scenario: Graves by custom
- **WHEN** a people burns its dead
- **THEN** its settlement has no graves but a pyre ground

#### Scenario: A signpost
- **WHEN** the player reads a signpost at a crossroads
- **THEN** it names the real places the roads lead to and their distances
