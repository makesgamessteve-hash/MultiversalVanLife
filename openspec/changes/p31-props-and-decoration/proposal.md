# Proposal

Phase 31 of 33 of the roadmap (see `docs/ROADMAP.md` and `docs/concepts/living-planets.md`). Depends on: p30.

## Why

Buildings and streets alone still look empty: the things of daily life, shaped by culture and customs, make a place feel lived in.

## What Changes

- Street furniture by technology: lamps (oil, lantern, magic, electric), benches, signposts in the native language with real destinations.
- Market stalls with the local food from the customs, carts with their draught animals, barrels, crates, sacks, washing lines.
- Graves by funeral custom, shrines and banners with faith symbols, statues.
- Decorations on holidays from the calendar (lanterns for a full-moon feast, flowers in spring).
