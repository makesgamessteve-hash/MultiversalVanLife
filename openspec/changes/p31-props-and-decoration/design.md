# Design

## Context

See `docs/concepts/living-planets.md`. This phase builds on p30.

## Decisions

- **Prop sets** per culture from technology, customs, faith and climate; placed along streets, at doors, in squares and yards by rules per lot and street kind.
- **Signposts** read the road network: destination names and distances in the people's language (glossed in the codex).
- **Holidays**: the calendar's holiday on the visit's date switches decoration sets.
