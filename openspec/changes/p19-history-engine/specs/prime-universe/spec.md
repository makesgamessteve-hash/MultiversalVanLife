# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Earth is a quiet world
Generated histories SHALL NOT place events, factions or individuals in the Prime universe; in the cosmos's lore Earth is a quiet world the rest of the multiverse has overlooked.

#### Scenario: No history in Prime
- **WHEN** histories are generated for any number of universes
- **THEN** none of their events refers to an address in the Prime universe
