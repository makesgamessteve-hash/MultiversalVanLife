# Proposal

Phase 14 of 24 of the procedural multiverse roadmap (see `docs/ROADMAP.md`). Depends on: p13.

## Why

Life should fit its world and later explain its peoples: every living planet needs its own tree of life, ecosystems in each biome and the intelligent species that will build cultures.

## What Changes

- Biochemistry and lineages (body plans) per living planet; species radiated into biomes with adaptations and ecological roles; names and bestiary entries.
- Intelligent species evolved from a lineage or committed from above, with physiology traits for later culture passes; colonists only where history places them.
- Chunks populated with plants and animals by biome and climate, drawn with tinted fallback sprites until phase 15.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `life-generation`: Trees of life, adaptation, ecosystems, intelligent species and their spread (behaviour follows in phase 15).

### Modified Capabilities
- `land-streaming`: Adds plants and wildlife.

## Impact

- `gen/life/*.lua` (biochemistry, lineages, species, ecology, sapients), chunk population, bible sections, checker rules, game tests.
